Six Taps to a Plan

A working prototype of onboarding for someone who does not know what they want to do in the gym. Two safety taps, four questions, then a session they can start today. Answer it and the plan at the end really is computed from what you pressed.

Every screen in the flow, in order. Tier 0 and tier 1 are first run. Tier 2 screens appear later, in context, once the user has a reason to care. Tier 3 is never asked, only worked out from what gets logged. Click any screen to open it in the walkthrough.
